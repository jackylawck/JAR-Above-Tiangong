# 🕊️ 和平利用太空與開源倫理規範 (Peaceful Exploration & Ethics Charter)

---

### 繁體中文

#### 1. 外層空間和平利用倡議 (Outer Space Treaty Principles)
本專案堅定擁護聯合國 1967 年《外層空間條約》（Outer Space Treaty）所確立之基本準則：**「探索和利用外層空間應為全人類之福祉，並專用於和平目的。」**
本專案之核心願景在於降低航太工程與天體力學之理解門檻，啟發下一代探索宇宙之好奇心，堅決反對太空軍事化與軌道武器化。

#### 2. 開源社會責任 (Open Source Social Responsibility)
* **教育平等**：免費提供予全球青少年、學生、教師及科普愛好者研究學習。
* **無惡意程式碼承諾**：專案絕不包含任何惡意掘礦程式、後門、資料劫持或未宣告之網路請求。

---

### English

#### 1. Adherence to the Outer Space Treaty (1967)
This Project aligns with the core tenets of the United Nations **Outer Space Treaty (1967)**: *"The exploration and use of outer space shall be carried out for the benefit and in the interests of all countries... and exclusively for peaceful purposes."*
The Project’s mission is to democratize orbital mechanics and aerospace education, fostering scientific inspiration in future generations while resolutely opposing the weaponization of outer space.

#### 2. Ethical Code of Conduct
* **Educational Equity**: Freely accessible to students, educators, and science enthusiasts globally.
* **Clean Code Guarantee**: Contains zero malware, cryptocurrency miners, hidden telemetry, or unauthorized background network requests.
